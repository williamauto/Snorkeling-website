Cheeca Rocks

Tour requiredBeginner-friendly

A shallow patch reef and protected Sanctuary Preservation Area southeast of Upper Matecumbe Key, known for resilient coral heads.

Depth
8–20 ft
Getting there
Boat or tour required; it's a NOAA Sanctuary Preservation Area.
Fee
None noted
Marine life
hogfish, green moray eels, grunts, jacks, parrotfish, sea turtles, brain coral, star coral

Alligator Reef (Lighthouse)

Tour requiredIntermediate

A reef marked by its 1873 lighthouse, about 4 nautical miles east of Islamorada, and one of the most biodiverse reefs in the Upper Keys.

Depth
5–25 ft
Getting there
Boat or tour required.
Fee
None noted
Marine life
500+ recorded species

Hen and Chickens Reef

Tour requiredBeginner-friendly

A shallow patch reef 10 minutes north of Islamorada, named for its cluster of coral heads.

Depth
10–25 ft
Getting there
Boat or tour required, off Plantation Key.
Fee
None noted
Marine life
purple coral, Christmas tree worms, star coral, brain coral, tropical fish

Founder's Park

Shore / walk-inBeginner-friendly

A county park at mile marker 87 with a protected swimming lagoon. Snorkeling here is limited, but it's the only true shore option in Islamorada.

Depth
3–8 ft
Getting there
Walk-in from the park's swimming area.
Fee
Free
Marine life
assorted juvenile fish

Tour requiredBeginner-friendly

Depth

Fee
