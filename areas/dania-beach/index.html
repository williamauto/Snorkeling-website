Dania Beach

A quieter stretch with one named reef near the Dania Pier.

Boat access only.Free beach access1 named spot

Best time to go

A calm-water day near slack tide gives the clearest look at the reef around the pier.

Getting there

Charters run out of Dania Beach's marinas just south of the pier; there is no shore route to the reef itself.

Erojacks Reef

A reef patch near the Dania Beach Pier with a good mix of shallow and mid-depth structure.

Depth
14–20 ft
Getting there
Boat access near the Dania Pier.
Fee
None noted
Marine life
nurse sharks, tarpon, angelfish, parrotfish, sea turtles
Tip: The mix of depths makes this a decent pick for a mixed group of snorkelers and divers on the same charter.
