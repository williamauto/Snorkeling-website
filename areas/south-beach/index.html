South Beach

Boat-access natural reef and ledges just south of Government Cut.

Boat access only; there is no shore snorkeling reef in South Beach proper.Free beach access3 named spots

Best time to go

Go on a slack-tide, low-current day since both sites sit in 25 to 30 feet of water near a shipping channel.

Getting there

Charters depart from marinas around South Pointe and the Miami Beach Marina, a short run to both sites.

South Pointe Park

A popular shore snorkeling spot at the southern tip of Miami Beach, near the jetty where Government Cut meets the ocean.

Depth
3–10 ft
Getting there
Wade in from the beach at South Pointe Park, the southern tip of Miami Beach.
Fee
None noted
Marine life
tropical reef fish, rays, crabs
Tip: Snorkel along the jetty rocks; check conditions - the cut can have current.
Source: Snorkeling in Miami - Miami & Beaches

Rainbow Reef

A natural reef south of Government Cut, deep enough that it rewards confident snorkelers or a paired dive-and-snorkel trip.

Depth
25–30 ft
Getting there
Boat access, about 3 miles offshore south of Government Cut.
Fee
None noted
Marine life
tropical reef fish, soft coral
Tip: At 25 to 30 feet, this suits stronger swimmers; ask your charter if it is usually paired with a dive stop.
Ledge
Boat accessIntermediate

RJ's Ledge

Limestone patch-reef outcroppings popular with local lobster divers, also snorkelable on calm days.

Depth
25–30 ft
Getting there
Boat access, offshore from South Beach.
Fee
None noted
Marine life
spiny lobster, moray eels
Tip: Best on a flat-calm day given the depth; choppy conditions make the ledge hard to see clearly from the surface.
Area sources: Miami Beach Dive & Snorkel Sites
