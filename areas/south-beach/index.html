Rainbow Reef

Boat accessIntermediate

A natural reef south of Government Cut, deep enough that it rewards confident snorkelers or a paired dive-and-snorkel trip.

Depth
25–30 ft
Getting there
Boat access, about 3 miles offshore south of Government Cut.
Fee
None noted
Marine life
tropical reef fish, soft coral

RJ's Ledge

Boat accessIntermediate

Limestone patch-reef outcroppings popular with local lobster divers, also snorkelable on calm days.

Depth
25–30 ft
Getting there
Boat access, offshore from South Beach.
Fee
None noted
Marine life

South Pointe Park

Shore / walk-inBeginner-friendly

A popular shore snorkeling spot at the southern tip of Miami Beach, near the jetty where Government Cut meets the ocean.

Depth
3–10 ft
Getting there
Wade in from the beach at South Pointe Park, the southern tip of Miami Beach.
Fee
None noted
Marine life
tropical reef fish, rays, crabs
