Fort Lauderdale Beach

A mix of one shore reef and several boat-access ledges running parallel to the beach.

Mostly boat access; one shore option.Free beach access; metered parking5 named spots

Best time to go

Calm, low-wind mornings give the best visibility on the offshore ledges; Vista Park's shore reef is swimmable most days the flag isn't red.

Getting there

Vista Park at the north end has free beach parking; boat trips to the ledges depart from marinas along the Intracoastal near Las Olas.

Vista Park Reef

A shore-entry reef at the quieter north end of Fort Lauderdale Beach, less crowded than the central strip.

Depth
11–15 ft
Getting there
Walk-in from Vista Park beach access.
Fee
Free
Marine life
corals, schooling fish, lobster, snapper, stingrays
Tip: Park at Vista Park itself rather than the central beach lots; the reef sits close to shore, so there is little swim-out.

Mixed access

Reachable more than one way, by tour, private boat, or kayak depending on conditions.

Reef
Mixed accessIntermediate

Hall of Fame Reef

Named for its proximity to the International Swimming Hall of Fame, a farther offshore reef better suited to confident swimmers.

Depth
15–30 ft
Getting there
Reachable by boat or a long kayak paddle, about 5 miles offshore.
Fee
None noted
Marine life
reef fish, ledge-dwelling species
Tip: The distance offshore means more boat traffic and current; go with a charter rather than attempting the paddle unless you are experienced.

Boat access

Reachable by private boat or a charter that will run you out and back.

Ledge
Boat accessIntermediate

Twin Ledges Reef

Two parallel limestone ledges a short run off Las Olas Beach.

Depth
12–22 ft
Getting there
Boat access near Las Olas Beach.
Fee
None noted
Marine life
nurse sharks, grouper, snapper, sea turtles
Tip: Ask your charter to anchor between the two ledges so you can drift over both without a long swim.
Reef
Boat accessIntermediate

Fisher's Pedestal

A reef structure off Sunrise Boulevard with enough depth range to suit both snorkelers on the shallow crest and divers below.

Depth
9–30 ft
Getting there
Boat access off Sunrise Boulevard.
Fee
None noted
Marine life
Nassau grouper, barracuda, tiger grouper
Tip: Stick to the shallow crest if you are snorkeling only; the deeper sections are really a dive site.
Ledge
Boat accessBeginner-friendly

Oakland Park Reef

Fractured limestone ledges off Oakland Park Boulevard, a short run north of central Fort Lauderdale Beach.

Depth
10–16 ft
Getting there
Boat access off Oakland Park Boulevard.
Fee
None noted
Marine life
spiny lobster, juvenile fish
Tip: A good beginner boat trip since it sits shallower than the other Fort Lauderdale ledges.
