Emerald Reef

Mixed accessBeginner-friendly

A shallow patch reef off Key Biscayne and one of Miami's most popular snorkel destinations thanks to its shallow, calm crest.

Depth
4–15 ft
Getting there
Reachable by tour boat, kayak, or private boat with a mooring permit; there is no walk-in access.
Fee
Bill Baggs Cape Florida SP: $8/vehicle if launching from the park
Marine life
parrotfish, angelfish, sergeant majors, soft coral

Half Moon Wreck

Boat accessBeginner-friendly

A 47-foot yacht that sank in 1930, sitting in shallow enough water for snorkelers as well as divers.

Depth
10–20 ft
Getting there
Boat access only.
Fee
Free
Marine life
puffer fish, spiny lobster, stingrays

Neptune Memorial Reef

Tour requiredIntermediate

An Atlantis-inspired artificial reef and underwater memorial about 3 miles east of Key Biscayne. At 40 feet deep, it suits confident snorkelers best, and most operators pair the trip with a dive.

Depth
35–40 ft
Getting there
Boat or tour required; gear rental is available through Tarpon Lagoon.
Fee
None noted
Marine life
tropical reef fish
