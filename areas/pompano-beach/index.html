Boat accessIntermediate

15–30 ft

Fee
None noted

Depth
15–30 ft
Getting there

Fee

Pompano Drop Off

Boat accessBeginner-friendly

A long reef line with over 25 mooring buoys, ledges, and holes. Usually very little to no current - a relaxed drift snorkel.

Depth
15–30 ft
Getting there
Boat access; a ridge complex with 25+ mooring buoys running parallel to shore.
Fee
None noted
Marine life
staghorn coral, tropical reef fish, lobster
Source: Pompano Drop Off - South Florida Diving Headquarters

Pompano Shore Reef

Shore / walk-inBeginner-friendly

One of the few places in South Florida where you can snorkel a natural coral reef straight from the beach.

Depth
3–10 ft
Getting there
Wade in directly from Pompano Beach - a natural coral reef just offshore.
Fee
None noted
Marine life
tropical reef fish, crabs, rays
Source: Pompano Beach - Visit Florida
